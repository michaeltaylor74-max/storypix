## Product name
StoryPix

## Description
StoryPix turns folders of photos and videos into a polished slideshow video with music — made for end-of-year school showcases, graduations, farewells, weddings, birthdays and family holidays.

Point StoryPix at a folder and add a song. Auto Build makes the whole show in seconds: each sub-folder (like "Camp" or "Sports Day") becomes its own section with a title slide, photos are sorted by the date they were taken, near-duplicate burst shots are skipped, and collages keep the pace up when there are hundreds of photos. If there are too many photos for the music, StoryPix says so plainly and offers choices.

Then make it yours, slide by slide. Change layouts with one click, drag photos in from the library, add captions and title cards, trim video clips to the best part, and zoom in on a face or a detail. Everything stays editable, with undo for every change and automatic saving.

When you're happy, export a standard MP4 in Full HD or 4K that plays on any projector, TV, laptop or YouTube.

StoryPix is free and open source: no account, no watermark, no ads and no in-app purchases. It works completely offline — your photos never leave your computer.

Made by a teacher, for teachers. Step-by-step guide and a two-minute walkthrough: https://getstorypix.com/guide.html

## Product features (one per line, up to 20)
Auto Build: turn a folder of photos and videos into a finished show in seconds
Sub-folders become sections with their own title slides
Fits the show to your music, or to a length you choose, and warns when the pace is too fast
Up to nine photos on a slide, with 25 layouts, frames, rounded corners and polaroid styles
Slow pan and zoom on every photo, plus Zoom to a spot for faces and details
Video clips with trimming, and music that dips automatically while a clip plays its sound
Captions and title cards with fonts, outlines and gentle animations
Transitions: crossfades, pushes, wipes and more
Photos are never stretched or squashed unless you choose
Autosave, crash recovery and undo for everything
Exports a standard MP4 in Full HD or 4K
Works offline; no account, no watermark, no ads
