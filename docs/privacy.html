Privacy

Last updated 31 October 2026

The StoryPix app

StoryPix works entirely on your computer. It does not collect, store or send any personal information, and it never uploads your photos, videos, music or shows anywhere.

  • No account, no sign-in, no advertising and no tracking.
  • No internet connection is used by the app. (On Windows, the installer from our website may download Microsoft's WebView2 component if your PC doesn't already have it.)
  • Files stay on your computer. StoryPix reads the photos, videos and music you choose, and writes the shows (.storypix files) and videos you save. It also keeps small preview copies of your photos and videos in its own folder on your computer so editing is quick. These are deleted automatically after 30 days unused, or straight away from Help → Clear preview copies.
  • Network drives: if a show uses files from another computer or a school server, StoryPix asks before opening them.

This website

getstorypix.com is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log basic technical information such as IP addresses for security; see GitHub's privacy statement. The download links go to GitHub, which counts how many times each file is downloaded.
